The Artist

A Decade of Wonder on Berlin's Stages

Ley has stood on stage since being a child — as a solo musician and performer, with their circus collective Entourage Berlin, as an accordionist in GRIPS theater and across bands from The Electronic Swing Orchestra to Frollein Smilla. Ley is a trained music therapist, a costume maker and professional multi-hoop artist with a special love for light magic.

Highlight Production · 20–30 min

Lucina's Light

A Spectral Symphony

Follow Lucina, the maker of light, through a fantastical universe guided by one small red glow. Precision hoop choreography and floating wands merge new circus with cutting-edge LED artistry — a journey of wonder for weddings, festivals and grand galas. Extended, video-mapped versions available for large façades.
