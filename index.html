Ley Lotta

Ley is a multiversatile artist from Hamburg, living in Berlin for over a decade. They have been performing as a musician and circus artist since childhood — solo, as well as with their performance collective Entourage Berlin and several band projects, including The Electronic Swing Orchestra and Frollein Smilla.

Ley is a songwriter and sound designer, a studied music therapist and bodywork therapist, and a professional multi-hoop artist with a special love for LED light equipment. Their circus focus lies in walking acts, elaborate costumes, multi-hoop artistry and refined LED performances with a range of tools.
