Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy one of your costumes?

A first small collection is in preparation. If you have seen a piece on this website that you would like, write to Ley — some pieces exist more than once or can be rebuilt.

What are the costumes made of?

All of them are designed and sewn by hand in Ley's Berlin studio: fabric, corsetry, headpieces, and for the illuminated pieces fibre optics and programmable LED elements.

Can you build a costume for my show?

Yes, commissions are possible — for stage, walking act or photo production. Tell Ley your idea, your deadline and your budget, and they will tell you what is realistic.
