Lucina's Light

A Spectral Symphony

Lucina's journey begins by moonlight in an enchanted forest. From there — guided by one small red glow — it leads through wondrous universes that let the borders of reality blur.

Precise programming, detailed choreography and refined sound design create a fairytale illusion that turns the entire stage into a distant, interactive dreamland: professional multi-hooping, high-end circus tools, hand-made LED costumes and the seamless merging of new circus, magic and poetic light art.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the LED show?

The full production runs about 30 minutes and is built from six acts. Short versions from ten minutes work as a highlight between other programme points, and single acts of three to six minutes are possible too.

How much space does the show need?

A stage or playing area from 4 × 5 metres and 3 metres of height. Bigger is always welcome.

Does the show work outdoors and in daylight?

The show is made for indoors and outdoors alike; the LED costumes and light props still need dusk or darkness to unfold their magic. For daytime events, themed walking acts or short pop-up hoop performances are the better choice — and, if you like, longer variety shows together with other artists from Ley’s pool of performers.

Can the show be made bigger for a large venue?

Yes. Lucina Extended adds projection mapping, developed together with a mapping specialist, for large halls, churches, façades and festival stages.

What does it cost to book the show?

The fee depends on several things: the date, the kind of event, the travel, the performance time and length, and how elaborate the programme should be. Send your event details through the contact form and you will get a quote, with no obligation.

Where do you perform?

Ley is based in Berlin and performs all over Germany — castle nights, light festivals, galas, weddings and company celebrations. International bookings are possible on request.
