Questions Before You Ask

How do I book you?

Through the form on this page or by email to info@leylotta.com. The more you tell Ley — date, place, time of day, occasion, indoor or outdoor, number of guests — the more precise the answer will be.

How far in advance should I ask?

Popular dates in the summer festival season and around Christmas go early, so a few months ahead is ideal. Short-notice requests are still worth sending: sometimes a date is free.

Do you travel outside Berlin?

Yes, all over Germany, and abroad on request. Travel and, for longer distances, accommodation are part of the quote so there are no surprises.

What do you need from the venue?

That depends on the act and the room. Once Ley knows where and when you are celebrating, they will send you a short, plain-language list of what is needed on site.
